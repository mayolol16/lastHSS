## Answer Key

### Section 1: Policy & Economics

**1. D** — Evidence base, social strategy, and political will are the three forces; market competition is not.

**2. B** — Employers, insurers, and governments are payers.

**3. A** — The Quintuple Aim adds health equity (and workforce well-being) to the earlier aims.

**4. B** — Deductible: what the patient pays before the plan begins to pay.

**5. A** — Co-pay: flat fee at the point of care.

**6. B** — Co-insurance: the patient's share after the deductible is met.

**7. C** — Cost = price × quantity; more services at the same price raises cost.

**8. D** — Private employer-sponsored coverage is not government-sponsored.

**9. A** — The Iron Triangle is cost, quality, and access; policymakers can usually optimize only two.

**10. C** — Public support for resources is political will.

**11. D** — Providers know far more than patients, violating the perfect-information assumption.

**12. D** — Demand is driven by need, not price, so patients cannot simply forgo care.
